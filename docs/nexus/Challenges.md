# NE Challenge Manager

## Name
```text
NE Challenge Manager
```

## Description (plain text, max 350 characters)
```text
Optional rules for harder SteamWorld Heist 2 runs, each with its own switch: no grind (no replays, no respawning loot), mutators (1 HP crews or enemies, every shot explodes, bots explode on death) and hardcore (no checkpoints, no quit-and-reload, permadeath, one life).
```

## Full description (BBCode)
```text
[size=5][b]Description[/b][/size]
NE Challenge Manager adds optional rules for harder runs of SteamWorld Heist 2. Every rule has its own switch, so you can play with just one of them or stack them all. Works on its own or together with NE Randomizer.

[size=5][b]Installation instructions[/b][/size]
[list=1]
[*]Install [b]NE Mod Loader & Manager[/b] first.
[*]Unpack this archive into the game folder (you get [i]SteamWorld Heist 2\Mods\Challenges\mod.json[/i]), or install it with Vortex or by dragging the .zip onto the loader.
[*]Close the game, open SWH2ModLoader.exe, tick [b]NE Challenge Manager[/b], choose your challenges and click [b]Apply to game[/b].
[/list]
No-grind settings can be changed mid-save; missions you have already won get locked as soon as the mod is installed. For permadeath and one-life runs, start a new game.
[b]Uninstall:[/b] untick it and click Apply, or click Restore original files.

[size=5][b]Main features[/b][/size]
[b]No grind[/b] — everything farmable becomes a one-time reward:
[list]
[*][b]Won missions can't be replayed[/b] — stored in your save. Story rematches stay open until their quest is done.
[*][b]Flagships and artifacts don't respawn[/b], no daily floating loot on the map.
[*][b]World map ships drop nothing.[/b]
[/list]

[b]Mutators[/b] — the whole game, every mission (procedural ones too):
[list]
[*][b]Crew have 1 HP[/b] — armor still works.
[*][b]Enemies have 1 HP[/b] — bosses and enemies whose script phases depend on HP keep theirs.
[*][b]Every shot explodes[/b] — yours and the enemies'; the blast radius is adjustable.
[*][b]Bots explode when scrapped[/b] — crew and enemies alike, chain reactions included.
[/list]

[b]Hardcore[/b]
[list]
[*][b]No checkpoints[/b] — "Load Checkpoint" is disabled in missions (patches the game exe).
[*][b]Quitting mid-mission forfeits it[/b] — no quit-and-reload to rewind a turn; continuing a mission from a save works like Abort.
[*][b]Permadeath[/b] — a crewmate scrapped in a mission is gone for good. The Captain is exempt.
[*][b]One life[/b] — losing a combat mission ends the run: every combat mission gets locked and you have to start a new game.
[/list]
Note: with the hardcore rules a game crash mid-mission counts as a loss too.

[size=5][b]Requirements[/b][/size]
[list]
[*][b]NE Mod Loader & Manager[/b] 1.1 or newer.
[*]SteamWorld Heist 2 (Steam version). "No checkpoints" patches the game exe and needs the current Steam build; the other rules work without it.
[/list]
Works together with my other mods. Source code: [url=https://github.com/nerdError/SteamWorldHeist2-Mods]GitHub[/url].
```
