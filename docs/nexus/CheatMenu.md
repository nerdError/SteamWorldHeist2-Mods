# NE Cheat Menu

## Name
```text
NE Cheat Menu
```

## Description (plain text, max 350 characters)
```text
In-mission cheats for SteamWorld Heist 2 from a window in the mod loader or hotkeys (Ctrl+Alt+1...8): win mission, heal crew, kill or weaken enemies, alarm off, give any item, plus toggles for invulnerable crew, frozen enemies and attacks that don't end your turn.
```

## Full description (BBCode)
```text
[size=5][b]Description[/b][/size]
The game's own debug menu was removed from the release build. NE Cheat Menu brings cheats back for missions: use the [b]Cheats…[/b] window in the mod loader or hotkeys while you play. Handy for testing, for getting past a mission you're stuck on, or just for fun.

[size=5][b]Installation instructions[/b][/size]
[list=1]
[*]Install [b]NE Mod Loader & Manager[/b] first.
[*]Unpack this archive into the game folder (you get [i]SteamWorld Heist 2\Mods\CheatMenu\mod.json[/i]), or install it with Vortex or by dragging the .zip onto the loader.
[*]Close the game, open SWH2ModLoader.exe, tick [b]NE Cheat Menu[/b] and click [b]Apply to game[/b].
[*]Click [b]Cheats…[/b] in the loader and keep the window open while you play. Hotkeys work while the game has focus.
[/list]
[b]Uninstall:[/b] untick it and click Apply, or click Restore original files.

[size=5][b]Main features[/b][/size]
[list]
[*][b]Win mission[/b] — Ctrl+Alt+1
[*][b]Heal crew[/b] — Ctrl+Alt+2
[*][b]Kill all enemies[/b] — Ctrl+Alt+3 (may break scripted boss fights)
[*][b]Enemies to 1 HP[/b] — Ctrl+Alt+4
[*][b]Alarm off[/b] — Ctrl+Alt+5
[*][b]Invulnerable crew[/b] (toggle) — Ctrl+Alt+6
[*][b]Freeze enemies[/b] (toggle) — Ctrl+Alt+7
[*][b]Attacks don't end turn[/b] (toggle) — Ctrl+Alt+8
[*][b]Give any item[/b] — from the window
[/list]
Cheats work inside missions; the window shows whether a mission is running. Toggles survive game restarts. There's also a command line: [i]swh2 cheat win[/i], [i]swh2 cheat god on[/i], [i]swh2 cheat give <item_id>[/i].

[size=5][b]Requirements[/b][/size]
[list]
[*][b]NE Mod Loader & Manager[/b] 1.0 or newer.
[*]SteamWorld Heist 2 (Steam version).
[/list]
Works together with my other mods. Source code: [url=https://github.com/nerdError/SteamWorldHeist2-Mods]GitHub[/url].

[size=5][b]Shout outs[/b][/size]
A huge thank you to the original developers of SteamWorld Heist 2 — the team at [b]Image & Form[/b], who were unfairly let go after the game's release. I truly love everything they have made, and this is my tiny contribution to the world they built. Thank you for these games.
```
