# NE Randomizer

## Name

```text
NE Randomizer
```

## Description (plain text, max 350 characters)
```text
A seeded randomizer for SteamWorld Heist 2: missions, item rewards (key items too, with logic), shops, enemies, weapon stats, perks, prices, world map, mission mutators, music, dialogue and more. Balanced and Chaos presets, both beatable. Same seed = same game, with a spoiler log.
```

## Full description (BBCode)
```text
[size=5][b]Description[/b][/size]
NE Randomizer turns SteamWorld Heist 2 into a new game every time. It shuffles missions on the world map, the game's fixed rewards, shop stock, enemies, weapon and ability stats, class perks, prices, the world map itself — and for fun: dialogue, music, names, looks and much more.

It is [b]seeded[/b]: the same seed and settings always produce the same game on any PC, so you can race friends or share a good seed. Every install writes a spoiler log with everything that changed.

Pick a preset and you're done:
[list]
[*][b]Balanced[/b] (default) — plays like the normal game: new enemies and gear in new places, but the same difficulty curve.
[*][b]Chaos[/b] — anything anywhere, but still beatable: stronger enemies come in smaller numbers, factions fight each other, late-game loot can show up early.
[/list]
Or build your own mix: every part has its own switch and its own Balanced/Chaos mode, and quick switches turn whole groups (main, stats, cosmetic) on or off at once.

[size=5][b]Installation instructions[/b][/size]
[list=1]
[*]Install [b]NE Mod Loader & Manager[/b] first.
[*]Unpack this archive into the game folder (you get [i]SteamWorld Heist 2\Mods\Randomizer\mod.json[/i]), or install it with Vortex or by dragging the .zip onto the loader.
[*]Close the game, open SWH2ModLoader.exe, tick [b]NE Randomizer[/b], pick a preset (and a seed if you want) and click [b]Apply to game[/b].
[*]Start a [b]new save[/b] for the full experience (in an existing save only places you haven't visited change).
[/list]
The spoiler log is in [i]SWH2ModLoader\Reports\Randomizer\[/i] — don't open it if you want surprises.
[b]Uninstall:[/b] untick it and click Apply, or click Restore original files.

[size=5][b]Main features[/b][/size]
[b]Progression[/b]
[list]
[*][b]Missions[/b] — world map markers start other missions, inside each sea (Balanced) or across the whole map (Chaos). Missions the story needs stay where they are.
[*][b]Item rewards[/b] — mission loot, bounty rewards, world map artifacts and quest rewards are swapped between places (by tier in Balanced, all together in Chaos).
[*][b]Story key items[/b] (optional) — can move with logic: only to places the story reaches earlier, so the game always stays beatable.
[*][b]Shops[/b] — stock swapped between shops (same kind and tier) or drawn from the whole game.
[/list]

[b]Battles[/b]
[list]
[*][b]Enemies[/b] — replaced with enemies from the whole game; Balanced gives them the stats of the enemy they replace, Chaos lets them keep their own (with limits), and they can keep their own faction and fight each other.
[*][b]Enemy guns and AI[/b], [b]enemy hats[/b], [b]explosive cover[/b].
[*][b]Mutator roulette[/b] — each mission gets its own modifiers (Double tap, Slippery, Volatile, Big booms…).
[*][b]Chaos events[/b] — at the start of a turn something may happen: adrenaline, a mutiny, a teleporter glitch, the alarm…
[*][b]Golden enemy[/b] — tougher, but drops a prize. [b]Regional edicts[/b] — every region has its own rule.
[/list]

[b]Stats[/b]
[list]
[*]Weapon damage, utility and ability numbers, crew health/cogs/move, prices — Balanced keeps things fair (e.g. a stronger utility costs more), Chaos changes each number on its own.
[*]Class perks move between classes; projectiles (lasers, lightning, fire…) move between weapons.
[*]Starting jobs, personal abilities, a random start, sub weapons, upgrade costs, loot decks.
[/list]

[b]World map[/b]
[list]
[*]Whirlpools lead elsewhere, world map loot, patrols and ambushes, spike rocks and mines — all following the story timeline so nothing becomes unreachable.
[/list]

[b]Just for fun[/b]
[list]
[*]Dialogue lines, battle barks, item and enemy names (in every language), music, mission lighting, weapon models, sounds, effects, voices, crew hats, NPC looks and optional "mystery items".
[/list]

[b]Seeds and spoilers[/b]
[list]
[*]Same seed + same settings = the same game. Each part has its own random stream, so turning one option on doesn't reshuffle the others.
[*]A spoiler log with every change is written for every seed.
[/list]
The full list of options with every detail is in the README.md inside the archive.

[size=5][b]Requirements[/b][/size]
[list]
[*][b]NE Mod Loader & Manager[/b] 1.1 or newer.
[*]SteamWorld Heist 2 (Steam version).
[/list]
Works together with my other mods (NE Turbo Battles, NE Challenge Manager, NE Cheat Menu, NE More Save Slots).
Source code: [url=https://github.com/nerdError/SteamWorldHeist2-Mods]GitHub[/url].
```
